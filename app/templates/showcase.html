5-Stage Zero-Trust Explainable Architecture

Open Live Interactive Lab
STAGE 1
Multi-Level Traffic Input

Captures 24 network attributes across L1 Packet headers/entropy, L2 Flow throughput/IAT, and L3 Session, Identity & Edge/IoT drift.

STAGE 2
Leak-Free Preprocessing

Applies stratified train-only median imputation and StandardScaler normalization to eliminate feature skew and data leakage.

STAGE 3
10-Model DL & ML Classification

Fuses 6 PyTorch Deep Learning networks (1D-CNN, Bi-LSTM, Transformer, GNN, ResMLP, Autoencoder) with 4 ensemble ML classifiers.

STAGE 4
SHAP Explainability Layer

Eliminates "black-box" uncertainty using TreeSHAP feature attributions, L1/L2/L3 multi-level contribution breakdown, and analyst narratives.

STAGE 5
Zero-Trust Decision Engine

Computes continuous Entity Trust Scores (0–100), maps threats to MITRE ATT&CK, and enforces automated micro-segmentation & session revocation.
